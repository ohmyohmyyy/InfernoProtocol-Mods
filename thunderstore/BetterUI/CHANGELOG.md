# Changelog

## 0.10.0

- Replace the genetics list with a compact, translucent DNA tree using native gene icons and descriptions.
- Add curved branches, subtle selection animation, mouse/controller navigation, pan, zoom and recentering.
- Support reduced motion and an independent genetics toggle; preserve original genetics data and crafting behavior.
- Handle startup before the genetics panel exists and restore the native panel on failure.

## 0.9.6

- Added an automatic compatibility dependency for Inferno Protocol's Unity 6 IL2CPP metadata.

## 0.9.5

- Simplified the Thunderstore page and moved detailed documentation to GitHub.

## 0.9.4

- Replaced the legacy black crafting-progress popup with an integrated fabrication card.
- Added live item fill, progress, percentage, and batch quantity feedback.
- Restored the ingredient view automatically after crafting finishes.
- Kept the game's original crafting timing and completion behavior intact.

## 0.9.3

- Fixed the inventory hand-crafting launcher disappearing after the crafting panel closes.
- Kept the hand-crafting launcher in its original game-owned hierarchy.

## 0.9.2

- Introduced the focused crafting-table interface overhaul.
- Improved recipe rows, categories, component analysis, quantity badges, and action states.
- Added remembered green, blue, cyan, violet, and amber palettes.
